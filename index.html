What ProDentim is

ProDentim is a strawberry-flavored chewable tablet sold through ClickBank. Each one contains a blend the maker puts at 3.5 billion probiotic bacteria, plus a few plant and mineral ingredients. You chew one slowly each morning and let it dissolve in your mouth.

The idea is different from most dental products. Toothpaste and mouthwash aim to remove bacteria. ProDentim aims to add helpful ones, on the theory that a balanced mouth microbiome supports healthier gums and fresher breath.

How it's meant to work

Your mouth hosts hundreds of bacterial species. Researchers increasingly think oral health depends on the balance between them, not just on killing "bad" bacteria. Some probiotic strains, especially Lactobacillus reuteri, have shown modest benefits for gum inflammation in small clinical studies.

The maker's claims go further than the research does. Probiotics can't fix cavities, reverse gum disease, or whiten teeth on their own. A fair expectation is a supporting role: something that may help gums and breath when combined with brushing, flossing, and regular checkups.

Ingredients

Lactobacillus paracasei
A probiotic strain studied for gum health. The maker also links it to clearer sinuses.
B. lactis BL-04
A branded strain mostly researched for immune and respiratory support; included to help balance mouth bacteria.
Lactobacillus reuteri
The best-studied strain here for oral use, with small trials showing reduced gum bleeding and plaque.
Inulin
A plant fiber that acts as food for good bacteria (a prebiotic).
Malic acid
A fruit acid found in strawberries; the maker says it helps keep teeth looking white.
Tricalcium phosphate
A calcium-and-phosphate mineral included for tooth support.
Peppermint
Flavor and freshness; peppermint oil has mild antibacterial properties.

The label lists it as non-GMO, gluten-free, and stimulant-free, made in an FDA-registered facility. Exact amounts per strain aren't published, which is common for blends but worth knowing.

Who it suits

Worth considering if you

  • Brush and floss well but still deal with bad breath or tender gums
  • Want to add a probiotic to an existing routine
  • Prefer a chewable over a capsule

Probably skip it if you

  • Have pain, swelling, or a cavity. See a dentist first
  • Are immunocompromised, pregnant, or on medication, without asking your doctor
  • Expect visible whitening in days

FAQ

Does ProDentim replace toothpaste?

No. Keep brushing twice a day with fluoride toothpaste and flossing. ProDentim is meant to sit alongside that routine, not replace it.

How long until I notice anything?

Most probiotic studies run for several weeks. Give it at least 30 days, which is still well inside the 60-day refund window.

Are there side effects?

Probiotics are generally well tolerated, though some people get mild bloating at first. Check with your doctor if you're pregnant, nursing, immunocompromised, or taking medication.
